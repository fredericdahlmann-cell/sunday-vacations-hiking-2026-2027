How to turn a proposal into a confirmed event
  1. Volunteer using “I’m interested”. Interest is not a confirmed appointment. Fred confirms the lead and co-host; keep the GitHub thread updated.
  2. Reconnoitre/download the current GPX. Confirm loop start/end, ascent, technical obstacles, total mileage including station access, alternative route and safe exits. Elevation gain is not yet verified; measure from the selected GPX before publishing.
  3. At 4–6 weeks: check closures, landowner/group rules, conservation restrictions, hunting notices in autumn, daylight, bookings and facilities. Set the final cap, leader and sweep, cancellation and late-arrival policy.
  4. At 2 weeks: check train/bus outward AND return itineraries for the actual date, transfer margins, tickets and engineering work. Publish exact train/platform only after verification. Keep a missed-connection plan; never rely on the last bus.
  5. Carpool: seats determine capacity. Confirm drivers, luggage space, pickup exit/pin, fuel/parking split and return commitment. Recommended cost formula: agreed actual fuel + parking divided by agreed contributors; driver inclusion must be agreed. No transport sale assumed.
  6. At 72 h and again the previous evening: review IRM warnings and local river/fire/access notices. Postpone exposed/ladder/river routes if wet rock, high water, ice or thunderstorms are likely. Forest/coast routes need a wind assessment too.
  7. Event day: offline route, charged phone/power bank, first-aid kit, leader/sweep headcounts, emergency access location and 112. Brief participants on pace, footwear, water, lunch and technical hazards. Do not publish participant medical or private contact data here.

Suggested staffing: one lead + one sweep for all outings; experienced leadership for technical walks. Proposed caps include hosts: 15 for standard routes, 10 for Le Hérou, Rochehaut and Nisramont. Increase only after a route assessment.

Participant equipment: grippy hiking footwear, rain layer, warm layer, food, water (typically 1.5–2 L; more in heat), sun protection in summer, headlamp as contingency. Poles help on slopes but must be stowed on ladders.

Sales, costs and event positioning

Use the selling angle on each card as a short event hook. These are community walks with a social pace, not guided tours with guaranteed professional interpretation. State pace, distance, obstacles and fitness expectation before accepting registrations. Short technical hikes must not be marketed as easy because of distance.

Pricing: the current Explorer / Voyager / Ambassador event entitlements have not been verified for this calendar. Confirm the applicable membership rule and any organizer fee before publication. Do not infer a fee from an old membership price. Separate organization fee, public transport, driver cost-sharing, admission and optional meals. The transport ranges are planning allowances, not fare quotes.

Most outdoor routes have no admission included in this plan; verify managed-site charges and group rules. Furfooz has an entrance charge. Zwin Nature Park, castle interiors, museums, meals and guided visits are optional extras and must be booked/priced separately. Cafés/toilets are not guaranteed along trails: bring picnic supplies and plan a toilet stop before the hike.

Publishing: place capped registrations and waitlist on one platform; use other channels for discovery. Do not open multiple independent RSVP counts. Travel costs and any paid optional extension require a clearly stated refund/cancellation policy before collection.

“Both” means a rail/bus/walk access corridor is identified, not a reserved service. “Conditional” adds an unresolved weekend connection, path condition, or long-transfer issue. “Carpool only (planned)” describes the selected event plan: it does not claim that no bus or demand-responsive transport exists anywhere nearby.
